## Verified problem and accounting definition

The engine reserves before each inference inside BEGIN IMMEDIATE and settles
usage and its reservation atomically. Its ordinary budget resets settled usage
at UTC midnight and ages open holds out of current admission after one hour.
A long tool-loop RPC therefore cannot be bounded by a check before the RPC or
by an assumed duration. Keep the existing product source/wheel and normal
budget semantics; add a temporary explicitly owned acceptance admission guard.

Read-only live inventory found127 prior open production reservations totaling
25,180,581 micro-USD and24 Mario reservations totaling4,820,134 micro-USD. These
are unresolved maximum reservations, NOT established charges or new test spend.
They must remain unchanged and visible; no hold deletion, release or repricing
is part of this task. A ceiling including all those historical maxima would
already exceed USD5 before one new request. Therefore the acceptance ceiling
means additional activity, with pre-existing reservation and settled-usage IDs
frozen atomically as an immutable baseline. Separately report that historical
exposure; never present the USD5 as a cap on it or on a provider invoice.

Every new reservation counts at its full authorized maximum regardless of age
or UTC date. Only a matching genuine engine settlement can replace that hold
with its actual recorded charge. Existing baseline calls remain historical even
if their receipts arrive later; no test operation retries them. A new unbound
usage row counts conservatively or refuses admission. Missing, malformed or
uncertain settlement never frees a new maximum. Actual overrun is recorded in
full and blocks further dispatch; settlement must never be rolled back to hide
an incurred charge. All fixed per-database allocations sum to at most5,000,000
integer micro-USD; activated allocations cannot be copied, reset or replenished.

## Persistent SQL and trusted maintenance boundary

Every reservation insertion must encounter at least one intact persistent
reservation-insert guard. Each such guard validates the complete frozen owned
schema and ledger/accounting state, so missing or modified owned objects refuse
while any effective insert guard remains. Include enforcement in the reservation
capture trigger as well as the redundant admission triggers: deleting the two
admission triggers alone must still refuse, rather than admit another maximum.
Read-only helper status/resume also refuses missing enforcement objects.

A privileged actor removing EVERY effective reservation-insert guard can disable
SQL-only admission even while usage/audit triggers remain. Trusted maintenance
must exclude that action, whole-store rollback at the same path, alternative
manifests and paid copies/hosts. No host-global registry or resistance to privileged
DDL removal of all effective insert guards is claimed. This is an explicit
acceptance limit, replacing the earlier unqualified partial/missing-guard claim;
review it before repairing/accepting M1. Ordinary single/multiple object drift
must be tested with at least one effective reservation guard remaining.
