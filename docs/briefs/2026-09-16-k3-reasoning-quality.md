# Explicit K3 reasoning versus disabled reasoning

The previous comparison sent `thinking: disabled` through the OpenRouter adapter.
This is confirmed by reconstruction of all 20 arrival wire hashes and their zero
reported thinking tokens. Catalog default effort did not apply. The question is
whether explicit maximum reasoning improves source-grounded quality enough to
justify its incremental cost before adding a verifier/model cascade.

Compare the same frozen business inputs on the same DigitalOcean provider under
three configurations: thinking disabled with existing role ceilings; disabled
with an 8192-token ceiling; adaptive thinking with explicit maximum effort and
an 8192-token ceiling. The middle arm separates output-budget effects from the
reasoning switch. Do not change prompts or silently replace failed responses.

Use all 20 positive arrival cases and a deterministic stratified subset of 20
previously held-out business episodes, each evaluated for memory and current-state
tasks: 60 role requests, 180 planned outputs. These are now exposed diagnostic
cases, not fresh held-out evidence of generalization. Count episode overlap and
report each cohort separately. Retain the corrected historical Opus comparator
without assuming it is truth or paying to regenerate it.

An isolated evaluation transport must transmit and record exact reasoning controls,
retain raw thinking metadata privately, price the full completion ceiling and
keep unknown costs reserved. Production defaults, profiles, prompts, billing
server and backlog state remain unchanged. No regex-based semantic decisions.

This separately authorized experiment has a USD10 cumulative local ceiling,
including compatibility checks and uncertain holds. Previous study liabilities
remain untouched; do not reset or spend its allowance. Account funding is an
independent prerequisite: initial OpenRouter balance was below USD1; the CTO replenished it to approximately
USD20.88 before compatibility checks. No automatic credit purchase. The protocol
repair and its bounded diagnostic attempts are documented in the execution plan.
